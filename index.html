Extra Features

Things that don't fit neatly into one stage but are worth knowing about.

💾

Resume your session

If the app closes, crashes, or your PC shuts down mid-work, your full progress is saved. You'll be asked to resume or start fresh next time you open it.

🗄️

Saved History

Every past conversion is saved locally on your computer with PPTX, PDF and Original PDF downloads. Last 50 are kept; you can delete history or the full app data.

🎨

Direct Colourize

Skip the whole pipeline and colourize a PDF straight from Import — active until you import a new file.

🌓

Themes

Several gradient accent themes plus dark/light mode. Your choice is remembered.

🔗

Auto-pair

Vision Engine automatically pairs Cover Page and Today's Goal slides in Grid Assembly.

📌

Retain Images

A safety area for pages you've undone from Removed Images — Redo sends them back to removed.
